## Safe Participation in Online Communities

:::warning Precautions
1. **Protect privacy**: Do not disclose personally identifiable information
2. **Verify information**: Not all shared information is accurate; prioritize professional sources
3. **Identify triggers**: If certain discussions make you uncomfortable, you can leave
4. **No substitute for professional help**: Community support is complementary, not a replacement
:::

## Setting Boundaries

- If the community makes you feel worse, step away temporarily
- Do not over-engage; set limits for yourself
- Remember: reading about others' severe experiences may trigger your symptoms
