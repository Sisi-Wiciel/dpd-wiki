# Depersonalization-Derealization Disorder Knowledge Base

Welcome to DPDR Wiki, a knowledge base focused on **Depersonalization** and **Derealization** disorders.

## What is DPDR?

DPDR stands for **Depersonalization-Derealization Disorder**. It is a widely misunderstood phenomenon where individuals experience:

- **Depersonalization**: Feeling detached from one's own thoughts, emotions, or body, as if observing oneself from outside
- **Derealization**: Feeling that the surrounding world is unreal, vague, or dreamlike
