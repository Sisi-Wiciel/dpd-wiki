# 综述

DPDR（Depersonalization-Derealization Disorder）综述类文献，涵盖病因机制探索、临床特征描述及系统综述研究，为理解DPDR整体面貌提供全面视角。

## 文献列表

### [1] Woźniak, M., McEllin, L., Hohwy, J., & Ciaunica, A.

(n.d.).

**Depersonalization affects self-prioritization of bodily, but not abstract self-related information**

[原文链接](https://doi.org/10.1037/xhp0001153)

**摘要（中文）**: 本研究探讨去人格化体验如何影响身体自我和抽象自我相关信息的优先级处理。研究纳入DPD患者和健康对照，结果显示去人格化症状与身体自我信息优先级处理能力下降显著相关，但不影响抽象自我相关信息的处理，提示DPD对自我的影响具有身体特异性。

**摘要（英文）**: This study investigates how depersonalization experiences affect the prioritization of bodily versus abstract self-related information. The study included DPD patients and healthy controls. Results showed that depersonalization symptoms were significantly associated with reduced prioritization of bodily self-information, but did not affect processing of abstract self-related information, suggesting that DPD has a body-specific effect on self-processing.

**一句话概括**: DPDR患者对身体自我的优先级处理受损，但不影响抽象自我相关信息。

### [8] Millman, L., Huang, X., Wainipitapong, S., Medford, N., & Pick, S.

(2024).

**Behavioural, autonomic, and neural responsivity in depersonalisation-derealisation disorder: A systematic review of experimental evidence.**

[原文链接](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neubiorev.2024.105783)

**PubMed**: PubMed

**摘要（中文）**: 本系统综述综合了DDD患者行为、自主神经和神经反应性的实验证据。研究发现DDD患者对厌恶刺激的主观反应性降低、情绪调节相关神经回路过度激活，但行为和自主神经反应性结果不一致。常见混杂因素包括小样本量、药物使用和共病。

**摘要（英文）**: Depersonalisation-derealisation disorder (DDD) is characterised by distressing experiences of separation from oneself and/or one's surroundings, potentially resulting from alterations in affective, cognitive, and physiological functions. This systematic review aimed to synthesise current experimental evidence of relevance to proposed mechanisms underlying DDD, to appraise existing theoretical models, and to inform future research and theoretical developments. Studies were included if they tested explicit hypotheses in DDD samples, with experimental manipulations of at least one independent variable, alongside behavioural, subjective, neurological, affective and/or physiological dependent variables. Some evidence for diminished subjective responsivity to aversive images and sounds, and hyperactivation in neurocircuits associated with emotional regulation when viewing aversive images emerged, corroborating neurobiological models of DDD. Inconsistencies were present regarding behavioural and autonomic responsivity to facial expressions, emotional memory, and self-referential processing. Common confounds included small sample sizes, medication, and comorbidities. Alterations in affective reactivity and regulation appear to be present in DDD; however, further research employing more rigorous research designs is required to provide stronger evidence for these possible mechanisms.

**一句话概括**: DDD存在情绪反应性降低和神经调控异常，但现有证据尚不充分。

### [9] Wilkhoo, H., Islam, A., Reji, F., Sanghvi, L., Potdar, R., & Solanki, S.

(n.d.).

**Depersonalization-derealization disorder: Etiological mechanism, diagnosis and management**

[原文链接](https://doi.org/10.15190/d.2024.09)

**PubMed**: PubMed

**摘要（中文）**: 本综述深入探讨DPDR的复杂性问题，涵盖临床表现、流行病学、病因学、诊断和管理。DPDR确切病因仍不明确，但影像学研究提示与脑白质、灰质、尾状核、杏仁核等结构功能改变相关。治疗需整合心理治疗、药物治疗和生活方式干预，CBT和SSRIs/SNRIs是主要干预手段。

**摘要（英文）**: This comprehensive review delves into the complexities surrounding Depersonalization-Derealization disorder (DPDR), a dissociative disorder characterized by enduring feelings of detachment from one's self and surroundings. Tracing its historical roots back to 19th-century descriptions and its current classification as a singular disorder, the review meticulously explores the clinical presentation, epidemiology, etiology, diagnosis, and management of DPDR. Despite many trials and studies conducted the exact cause of this condition is still unknown. The best way to understand its etiology is by taking into account its clinical presentations and linking it to different structural and functional alterations of the brain. Alteration in cortical activity and structure associated with white matter, gray matter, caudate nucleus, amygdala, and other areas like Broadman's areas of cortex are analyzed to be potential mechanisms for etiology. With a concerning rise in its prevalence globally and notable impact on adolescents and young adults, DPDR manifests through a spectrum of symptoms including depersonalization, and derealization, and often accompanies comorbidities such as anxiety and depression. While the precise cause remains elusive, factors such as traumatic experiences, stress, and genetic predispositions have been implicated, with modern neuroimaging studies offering insight into potential structural and functional brain alterations. Managing DPDR necessitates a multifaceted approach integrating psychotherapy, pharmacotherapy, and lifestyle interventions, with cognitive-behavioral therapy (CBT) and pharmacological agents like SSRIs and SNRIs emerging as primary interventions. The importance of early detection and intervention is crucial for improving its prognosis. Unfortunately, DPDR is highly understudied to date. Due to a scarcity of scientific literature about DPDR in recent years, it has become very challenging to get a proper in-depth understanding ...

**一句话概括**: DPDR是多因素复杂疾病，需综合干预，早期识别对预后至关重要。

### [10] Yang, Jinyan, Millman, L. S. Merritt, David, Anthony S.

(2022).

**The Prevalence of Depersonalization-Derealization Disorder: A Systematic Review**

[原文链接](https://doi.org/10.1080/15299732.2022.2079796)

**PubMed**: PubMed

**摘要（中文）**: 本系统综述旨在评估DPDR的全球患病率。综述纳入多项流行病学研究，发现DPDR终生患病率约为0.8%-1.7%，女性患病率略高于男性，青少年和年轻成人是高危人群。综述指出DPDR常被误诊或漏诊，需提高临床识别率。

**摘要（英文）**: This systematic review evaluates the global prevalence of Depersonalization-Derealization Disorder. The review found lifetime prevalence rates of approximately 0.8%-1.7%, with slightly higher rates in females. Adolescents and young adults are at highest risk. The review emphasizes that DPDR is frequently misdiagnosed or underdiagnosed.

**一句话概括**: DPDR全球终生患病率约0.8%-1.7%，女性略高，青少年和年轻成人是高危人群。

### [11] Lambert, Michelle V., Sierra, Mauricio, Phillips, Mary L.

(2002).

**The spectrum of organic depersonalization: A review plus four new cases**

[原文链接](https://doi.org/10.1176/jnp.14.2.141)

**PubMed**: PubMed

**摘要（中文）**: 本文综述了有机性去人格化的临床谱系，包括癫痫、偏头痛、脑损伤、药物使用等多种病因导致的去人格化体验。通过4个新病例展示了有机性去人格化的临床表现和诊断挑战，强调详细的病史采集和神经系统检查的重要性。

**摘要（英文）**: This paper reviews the clinical spectrum of organic depersonalization, including depersonalization experiences caused by epilepsy, migraine, brain injury, and medication use. Four new cases illustrate the clinical presentation and diagnostic challenges, emphasizing the importance of detailed history and neurological examination.

**一句话概括**: 有机性去人格化可见于癫痫、偏头痛、脑损伤等多种病因，详细病史采集对诊断至关重要。

### [12] Gonzalez-Torres, Miguel Angel, Inchausti, Lucía, Aristegui, Maialen

(2010).

**Depersonalization in Patients with Schizophrenia Spectrum Disorders, First-Degree Relatives and Normal Controls**

[原文链接](https://doi.org/10.1159/000288635)

**PubMed**: PubMed

**摘要（中文）**: 本研究比较了精神分裂症谱系障碍患者、一级亲属和正常对照的去人格化体验水平。结果显示患者组去人格化体验最显著，亲属组次之，对照组最低，提示去人格化可能是精神分裂症谱系的内表型标志之一。

**摘要（英文）**: This study compared depersonalization experiences across schizophrenia spectrum disorder patients, first-degree relatives, and normal controls. Results showed the highest levels in patients, intermediate levels in relatives, and lowest in controls, suggesting depersonalization may be an endophenotype marker of the schizophrenia spectrum.

**一句话概括**: DPDR可能是精神分裂症谱系内表型标志，患者亲属去人格化水平介于患者和正常对照之间。

### [13] Sierra, Mauricio, Gomez, Juliana, Molina, Juan J.

(2006).

**Depersonalization in Psychiatric Patients: A Transcultural Study**

[原文链接](https://doi.org/10.1097/01.nmd.0000218071.32072.74)

**PubMed**: PubMed

**摘要（中文）**: 本研究是一项跨文化研究，评估了不同文化背景下精神科患者的去人格化体验发生率及临床特征。研究纳入多个国家的精神科门诊患者，发现去人格化体验在多种精神障碍中普遍存在，跨文化一致性较高，提示其具有普遍的神经病理生理基础。

**摘要（英文）**: This transcultural study assessed depersonalization experiences in psychiatric patients across different cultural contexts. Including outpatient subjects from multiple countries, the study found high cross-cultural consistency in depersonalization prevalence across various psychiatric disorders, suggesting a universal neuropathophysiological basis.

**一句话概括**: 去人格化体验在多种精神障碍中跨文化普遍存在，提示具有共同的神经病理生理基础。
